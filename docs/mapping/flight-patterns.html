How to read it: Flight A gives the orthophoto and DSM. B and C look at the same ground from four directions (camera faces the direction of travel, so alternate lines face opposite ways). Obliques remove the "bowl" doming of nadir-only grids and show walls for the mesh and the splat. For a flat map only, fly A + B. For 3D and splats, fly all three.
