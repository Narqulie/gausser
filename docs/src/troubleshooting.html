Symptoms, their causes and the fix. Most of these cost hours to find once.

First: gausser status and the monitor

gausser status lists missing tools and out-of-date steps. gausser monitor shows every step's log, the COLMAP cameras and the splat. Failed attempts stay in .gausser/tmp/.

Frames and capture

symptomcausefix
splat is soft or hazy everywherethe input lacks detail (resolution, motion blur, extraction scale)check the frames first; reshoot at 4K or as photos; do not train longer
splat from several people's videos is blurred or doubledone shared camera model for different phones (before 2026-11-17)re-run solve: frames of each video now get their own camera
solve registers only some photos of a phone set; the log shows CAMERA_SINGLE_DIM_ERRORphotos of two sizes (48 MP and 12 MP) shared one camera model (before 2026-11-19)re-run solve: each size and lens now gets its own camera
a model georeferenced from phone GPS is tilted (ground slopes, mesh leans)GPS along a walk cannot fix the roll about it; the full fit was 37° off on a 14 × 2 m walk (before 2026-11-19)re-run solve: it keeps the cameras' up when the GPS fit tilts it over 10°
frames: fewer than --min-framesshort clip or little movement--spacing high, or --every N
frames fails on the filter graphffmpeg older than 7 (-/filter_complex)brew upgrade ffmpeg
360°/QooCam splat is mirroredthe stored lens images are mirroredframes flips them on ingest; re-run frames with this version
hundreds of BITMAP_ERRORs on an exFAT driveAppleDouble ._* sidecarsgausser passes COLMAP an image list; update gausser

Training (Brush, Msplat)

symptomcausefix
Brush stalls or the Mac freezesunbounded splat growth on Metalkeep --max-splats at 1.5M
Brush trains on stale cameras or imagesit scans every file in its input foldergausser gives it a staging folder; never pass it the scene root
an unattended run prints nothingBrush logs only at info levelgausser sets RUST_LOG=brush_cli=info unless you set RUST_LOG
masked-out areas not transparentmasks make Brush ignore pixels, not train them transparentexpected; use -- --alpha-mode transparent if you want that
dense init keeps background in a masked runthe dense cloud ignores masks--init auto already uses sparse points for masked runs
Msplat black areasno mask input: masked images are composited onto blackprefer Brush for masked or --miss drop scenes
training paused overnightthe Mac slept (lid closed)gausser runs caffeinate; attach a display or keep the lid open
