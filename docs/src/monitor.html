A read-only web page that shows every scene gausser has worked on: the running step and its live log, the COLMAP cameras and points as the mapper adds them, the splat as Brush trains it, meshes, statistics and held-out renders. It never drives the pipeline and never takes the scene lock.

gausser run --prompt statue --monitor   # start or reuse the monitor, then run
gausser monitor                         # open the current scene
gausser monitor --all                   # the list of all scenes

One monitor (port 8737, recorded in ~/.local/share/gausser/monitor.json) serves every scene in the index. A monitor started by --monitor quits 10 minutes after its last tab closes when nothing runs. Screenshots below are real captures of scenes on this Mac, two of them while Brush was training.

The scene header

One cell per step: count, tool, state (up to date, out of date, running with progress and time left). The command line of the running step is on the right. "notify me when a step ends" adds a desktop notification to the toast.

3D tab

  • model: the newest COLMAP model, or a mapper snapshot while solve runs.
  • splat and compare with: any export of any run, two side by side.
  • mesh: a gausser mesh result, alone or over the splat, in the same frame.
  • Click a camera to see its photo or look through it.
  • Draw a crop box: a gizmo in the level frame; the page shows the gausser crop --box=… --up=… command to copy.
  • The reconstruction panel: cameras, points, reprojection error, mean track length.

How it works

monitor.py serves the built page from src/gausser/web/ and a JSON API on 127.0.0.1: state, images, frames, model, evals, log, thumb, file, scenes. Every request names its scene (?scene=); folders not in the index are refused, and paths outside the scene are refused. Live data comes from the files a step is writing: Brush's .gausser/tmp/brush-*/exports/*.ply and the COLMAP mapper's snapshots/. A running step is the lock file's pid being alive (and started before the lock was written). Log parsing for progress and charts lives in web/src/parse.ts.
